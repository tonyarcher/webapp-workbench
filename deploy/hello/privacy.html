Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-14 · Back to the gateway

These apps are a personal system, self-hosted on a home network and run by the operator for a small group of people they know. There is no advertising, no analytics, no third-party tracking, and nothing is sold or shared. Your data stays on the operator's server.

What each app stores

  • Accounts — your username, a password hash (Argon2), an optional authenticator-app secret, passkey credentials, and session and login-token hashes. Sign-in uses an HTTP-only cookie that expires after 12 hours.
  • RSS Reader — your feed subscriptions, downloaded article text, reading state, and optional AI summaries computed on the server by a configured backend.
  • Stock Game — your portfolio, trades, and game state. Market quotes are fetched from Yahoo Finance by the server.
  • Pulse 101 — the radio stations you add and your listening schedule. Audio plays straight from each station's own stream host.
  • Fitness — the activity samples you import from Health Connect on your phone.
  • Calendar Sync — its Trakt and Google connections. Trakt credentials and tokens are stored server-side and only ever sent to Trakt; the Google connection runs in your browser, where its access token stays in local storage and is only sent to Google. The app writes events into your own Google Calendar.
  • Baseball, Basketball, Football, Clipstack, Lemmy Vertical Scroll — entirely in your browser (local storage on your device). The server only serves their files.

External services these apps contact

  • Google (sign-in and Calendar) for Calendar Sync.
  • Trakt for Calendar Sync's watched-data sync.
  • Google Fonts for Baseball and Basketball typography.
  • Public CORS proxies (allorigins.win, corsproxy.io) when the RSS Reader cannot fetch a feed directly.
  • Yahoo Finance for Stock Game quotes.
  • The Lemmy or PieFed instances you subscribe to, and TikTok's oEmbed endpoint for links you add, in the respective apps.
  • The stream host of each station you add, when Pulse 101 plays audio.

Logs

The gateway keeps standard operational request logs (path, status, timing) on the server. Nothing is shipped to an external log service.

Your data

Ask the operator to export or delete anything stored about you; deletion removes the account and its data. This policy is kept in the deployment repository and updated when the stack changes.
