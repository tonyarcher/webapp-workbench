## How to read it

Adopt means the repo proves it. Trial means one app proves it this quarter.
Assess means watch it. Hold means the repo already rejected it.

Every Adopt item names the gate that enforces it. A rule without a gate
leaves the radar. The app catalog lives in apps.json. The formatter and
lint list lives in tools/verify.py. Do not copy either one here.

## Trial process

One Trial runs in one app per quarter. Name the app, the gate, and the exit
test before starting. Promote to Adopt or drop it at quarter end. No
standing Trials. The oxfmt Trial named above is the stock-game shared
workspace; its exit test is an empty oxfmt diff there plus a passing
verify.py.

## What moves an item

The quarterly review reads the Jev JSONL logs and the CI history, not
opinions. A rule with a bad hit rate drops one ring. A Trial with a passing
exit test rises. Anything without a log entry leaves the radar.
